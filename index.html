Our story

Small batches, honest ingredients, baked the same way since day one.

M’s by Marjorie’s started out filling small requests from neighbors and coworkers. The recipes haven’t changed since — real butter, toasted nuts, fruit folded in by hand — only the number of boxes going out the door on a good week.

Every cake, loaf, and pie is still made to order in Parañaque, ready for pickup or delivery.
