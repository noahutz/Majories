Message to Order

  1. Message us on Viber or Messenger with these details:

    • Name
    • Address
    • Order
    • Date of delivery/pick‑up
    • Preferred mode of payment

    Please allow at least 2 days to order cakes.

    Pick‑up is available at Robinsons Galleria or Merville, Parañaque.

  2. We’ll acknowledge your order within the day and send the total bill — delivery fee (Grab or Lalamove) included, if applicable.

  3. Deposit the total amount to the bank and send us the deposit slip.

  4. Once payment is confirmed: we’ll deliver your order, or you may pick it up at your preferred location.
